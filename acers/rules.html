Rules & Scoring

This page explains the structure used by the historical 2025/26 Series archive. Confirmed 2026/27 rules can replace this archive guide once the new season is finalised.

01

Series Points

Riders score race points in each round. For the Autumn 2025 final Series Standings, a rider's best three race scores count towards the overall total.

02

Sprint & KOM

Designated prime segments award separate Sprint and KOM points. These have their own season-long classifications alongside the overall Series Standings.

03

General Classification

GC is determined by cumulative time across the series. The historical results workflow applies the series rules for missing races when calculating a complete GC.

04

Corps Competition

Corps scores are accumulated race by race and displayed as a separate Corps Series Standing.

05

Verification

Membership verification is public on rider profiles. A rider who is not verified can see why their participation does not appear or count in official Series results.

06

Championships

The Army eRace Championship is a separate competition. Its results are labelled Championship Results and Championship Standings, not Series Standings.
